# Glossary: Commit City

- **City**: the whole 3D world on the page.
- **Landmark**: one of 8 buildings marked as one of Lincoln's projects. It has a hologram above it and opens a panel.
- **Hologram**: the floating wireframe symbol with light ribbons above a landmark.
- **Panel**: the curved hologram screens that unfold around the visitor at a landmark, with a flat copy on phones and for screen readers.
- **Stats bar**: the top-right readout: AI tokens, commits, top language, "as of" date.
- **Locked zone**: one walled district that stands for all other repos, with no names and no per-repo towers.
- **Movement mode**: one of five ways to move: first person, walk, drive, drone, orbit.
- **Quality tier**: the set of settings chosen for a device: desktop full, desktop reduced, phone lite.
- **City data**: the numbers file the PC job writes for the page.
- **PC job**: the scheduled job on Lincoln's PC (in the lstuek profile repo) that counts activity and uploads numbers only.
- **First block**: one street corner at full quality, built before the rest for Lincoln's review.
